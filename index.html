About this tool

What it does: Walks you through assembling a verified LoRa antenna chain from one Rokland device to one antenna, picking only catalog pieces that physically mate at each step.

Scope:

  • LoRa antenna chains only. BLE, WiFi, GPS, and cellular antennas ship pre-integrated inside the device. Replacing them is a teardown / repair-level operation (open the enclosure, swap a chip antenna or short pigtail) that this tool doesn't try to plan for.
  • Mechanical fit (connector mating) is verified. SMA, RP-SMA, N, and IPEX follow same-family + opposite-gender rules. IPEX uses industry naming: PCB-side = Male, cable-side = Female.
  • Band match is advisory. An EU 868 MHz antenna on a US 915 MHz device will fit mechanically but is flagged as a band-mismatch warning, not a block.
  • Form-factor sanity is advisory. A directional yagi on a handheld device warns you (you'll still get the chain).

What's out of scope: VSWR math, polarization compatibility, link-budget calculations, regulatory marking (FCC vs CE/ETSI), and any antenna upgrade for sealed/integrated radios.

Source: Rokland's product catalog, scraped + curated, validated against schema on load. Connector ontology collapses U.FL / IPEX1 / MHF1 (the original Hirose mini connector) into a single IPEX family. MHF4 / IPEX4 is a different connector and is filed under other-specialty.
